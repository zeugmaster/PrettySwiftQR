Mass

Nothing vanishes

Every patch of the code is a drop with a mass. A drop that reaches the target shape shrinks only as a bump of the same mass rises where it landed, and the shape's width follows the square root of the mass per length, the way a bead of liquid does. Until the last drop has landed the shape is only as complete as the mass that has arrived.

Threads

Pulled apart, not cut

Neighbouring patches are joined by threads anchored at the cells where they touched. As the drops move apart the thread stretches, thins and beads up in the middle, then snaps: both stubs recoil into their drops with a growing blob at the tip, and the bead is left behind as a satellite drop that is swallowed back by the heavier parent.

Magnets

Appear and disappear

To disappear, the code is pulled toward a sparse scatter of invisible attractors: patches melt into drops, flow to the nearest one, merge with whatever arrived there, and sink. To appear, the same film runs backwards: beads surface, split, and spread into the modules. Drops are nearly circular, elongate only with speed, and relax with a quick decaying wobble.
